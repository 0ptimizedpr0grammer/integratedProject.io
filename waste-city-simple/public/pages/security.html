How it works in WasteCity

AES-256-CBC generates a fresh random 16-byte IV per encryption. The IV is prepended to the ciphertext as iv_hex:encrypted_hex so decryption is self-contained. Zone notes and transcript content are encrypted with this scheme before being stored in SQLite.

How it works in WasteCity

Every waste record stores a SHA-256 hash of cityId + zoneId + date + wasteKg. Tampering with any field is detectable by recomputing and comparing the hash. All audit log entries store a SHA-256 hash of the request payload — the raw payload is never written to disk.
